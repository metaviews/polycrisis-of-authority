# White House AI Office

The White House AI policy function has cycled through several organizational forms, with the most recent being the White House AI Action Office and various task forces (OSTP's AI Subcommittee, the National AI Initiative Office within the Office of Science and Technology Policy). Across administrations, the function coordinates federal AI policy across agencies (NIST, NSF, NIH, DOD), represents the US in international coordination forums (OECD, G7, AI summits), and serves as the contact point for AI-related memos and executive orders.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus treats the White House AI Office as the principal reference for "executive-branch coordination on AI policy" — including executive orders (such as 14110 on Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Development and Use of AI), AI safety institutes coordination, and procurement-policy leverage. The corpus uses the office as a procedural reference rather than an advocacy one.
