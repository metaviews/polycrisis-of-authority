# Inflection AI

Inflection AI is the AI lab founded in 2022 by Reid Hoffman (LinkedIn co-founder), Mustafa Suleyman (co-founder of DeepMind, later at Microsoft AI), and Karén Simonyan (DeepMind researcher behind AlphaFold's protein-structure work). The company launched "Pi," a personal-AI chatbot positioned around emotional intelligence and conversational warmth, in 2023. In March 2024, Microsoft hired most of Inflection's team (including Suleyman, who became EVP and CEO of Microsoft AI) for ~$650 million; the company pivoted to enterprise B2B infrastructure (Inflection for Workloads, with access to Microsoft Azure compute). Inflection's case is one of the cleanest examples of the post-2023 frontier-lab consolidation: when capital, compute, and distribution become gating constraints, smaller independent labs are absorbed into the platform players.
