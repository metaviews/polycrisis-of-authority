# Zhipu AI

Zhipu AI (officially Knowledge Atlas Technology, founded 2019 in Beijing) ships the GLM family of models — including the GLM-4 series and the text-to-video CogVideoX line — and is part of the chinese frontier-lab cohort. Zhipu's distinguishing positioning is its deep integration with Chinese state-owned enterprise and government deployments, particularly in regulated industries where foreign-company models face compliance constraints. The company's role in the chinese AI ecosystem combines model development with a distinct sovereign-AI distribution channel.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus references Zhipu alongside Alibaba, DeepSeek, Moonshot, and other chinese-frontier forces as one of the operational alternatives to US-anchored AI models for Chinese-regulated-environments and aligned foreign deployments. The text-to-video dimension (CogVideoX) makes Zhipu a reference for AI-generated media policy discussions that span the chinese and global conversations.
