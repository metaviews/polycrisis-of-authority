# xAI

xAI is the AI lab founded by Elon Musk in March 2023, with the publicly stated mission "to understand the universe." The company ships the Grok family of models, deployed primarily via the X social platform as a chatbot with characteristically adversarial / humorous settings. xAI's relationship to OpenAI is the structural context: Musk co-founded OpenAI in 2015, departed the board in 2018, and the founding of xAI reflects a continuing critique of OpenAI's commercial alignment. xAI is one of the frontier-lab cohort in capability terms (large-scale training infrastructure, parameter count at frontier scale), with a distinct product strategy (X integration, real-time social data, less-restricted default personality).

## In the Polycrisis corpus

Coverage focuses on xAI's distinct personality framing as a model deployment choice and on its vertical integration with X (data access, distribution, billing). The corpus uses xAI as a reference point in the "platform-strategy" frame and as an example of personality-framing as product differentiation.
