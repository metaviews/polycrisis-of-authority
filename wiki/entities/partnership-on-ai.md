# Partnership on AI

The Partnership on AI (originally founded 2016 as a partnership between Apple, Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Facebook, and IBM; expanded significantly since 2018) is a multi-stakeholder nonprofit focused on advancing the understanding and responsible practice of AI. The Partnership's work includes research on algorithmic fairness, AI safety, transparency, and the deployment of AI systems in high-stakes contexts. Its institutional positioning — bringing together industry, civil society, and academic participants — gives it a distinct voice in the AI policy conversation.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus references Partnership on AI as the principal institutional reference for the multi-stakeholder governance frame, distinct from both government-led bodies (EU AI Office, NIST, AISI) and industry-only coalitions (Frontier Model Forum). The corpus uses the Partnership when simulations need to depict the breadth of actors engaged in AI governance rather than the binding layer.
