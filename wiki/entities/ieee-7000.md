# IEEE Ethically Aligned Design

The IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is the world's largest technical-professional organization with substantial standards-body work on AI. The **IEEE 7000-series** of standards covers AI ethics specifics: 7001 (transparency), 7002 (data privacy), 7003 (algorithmic bias considerations), 7009 (fail-safe design of autonomous systems), 7010 (wellness and wellbeing metrics for AI). The earlier **Ethically Aligned Design** framework document (Version 2, 2019) informed several subsequent standards work. The IEEE's positioning is technical, multi-stakeholder, and voluntary; the standards are referenced by procurement teams and compliance frameworks rather than regulatory bodies.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus uses IEEE as a reference for "technical standards work" on AI — distinct from policy-level frameworks (NIST AI RMF, OECD AI Principles, EU AI Act) and applicable in procurement contexts where technical-standards traceability matters more than regulatory compliance.
