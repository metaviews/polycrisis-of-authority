# UN AI Advisory Body

The UN Secretary-General's High-Level Advisory Body on Artificial Intelligence was established in October 2023 with a mandate to make preliminary recommendations on international AI governance by September 2024. The 39-member body's final report, "Governing AI for Humanity," recommended establishing an international scientific panel on AI (parallel to the IPCC for climate), a fund for AI governance capacity in developing countries, and a global AI fund for the same purpose. The body's recommendations are non-binding and have informed subsequent G7/OECD/G20 AI discussion tracks.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus references the UN Advisory Body as one of the principal multilateral frameworks for AI governance analysis, alongside the OECD AI Policy Observatory and the G7 Hiroshima AI Process. The corpus cites the body's reports when simulations need a "global cooperation" framing rather than a competitive-jurisdiction framing.
