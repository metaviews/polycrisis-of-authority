# China MIIT

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) is the principal Chinese cabinet-level ministry for industrial and information policy. Within the AI policy domain, MIIT — together with the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) — administers the **Provisional Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services**, effective August 2023, which requires Chinese-providers of generative AI services to obtain a license, implement content moderation, and comply with "core socialist values" content requirements before making services available to the public. Together with MIIT's broader "interim measures" framework, this is the operational regulatory regime for Chinese AI deployment.
