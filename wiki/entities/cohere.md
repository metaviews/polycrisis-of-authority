# Cohere

Cohere is a Canadian AI lab founded in 2019 by Aidan Gomez, Ivan Zhang, and Nick Frosst (with Gomez being a co-author of the "Attention Is All You Need" transformer paper). The company focuses on enterprise-grade natural language processing, with flagship products centered on text generation, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and embedding models for search. Cohere's positioning differs from consumer-frontier labs: where OpenAI and Anthropic ship consumer-facing ChatGPT and Claude products, Cohere's customers are enterprise teams who integrate the models into their own products.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus uses Cohere as a reference for the enterprise-deployment framing of AI policy — the distinct regulatory, procurement, and accountability dynamics that emerge when AI systems are deployed inside customer organizations rather than as consumer products. Cohere is also a reference for the "sovereign AI" deployment theme: as a Canadian-founded, Toronto-headquartered company with strong European partnerships, Cohere sits in the broader narrative of non-US frontier alternatives.
