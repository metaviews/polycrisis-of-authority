# Moonshot AI

Moonshot AI is the Beijing-based AI lab founded in 2023 by Yang Zhilin (formerly a researcher at Google Brain / Carnegie Mellon) and other co-founders with prominent academic AI backgrounds. The company ships the Kimi family of language models, with a publicly stated emphasis on long-context processing — Kimi was the first widely available Chinese consumer-facing AI product to advertise a long-context window of 1 million+ tokens (released in 2024). Moonshot is one of the four principal Chinese frontier-lab forces alongside Alibaba/Qwen, DeepSeek, and Zhipu.
