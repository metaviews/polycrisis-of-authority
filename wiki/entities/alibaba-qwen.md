# Alibaba Qwen

The Qwen model family is the AI language-model product line of Alibaba Cloud, one of China's largest cloud providers and the public cloud arm of the Alibaba Group. The Qwen series is the broadest open-weight output of any Chinese cloud provider, with multiple model sizes, vision-language variants (Qwen-VL), code-specialized variants (Qwen-Coder), and tuned chat variants. Qwen is the principal Chinese alternative to DeepSeek for open-weight adoption, particularly outside the United States in markets that prefer the Alibaba Cloud distribution channel.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus references Alibaba Qwen alongside DeepSeek and other Chinese frontier outputs as a significant capability-and-distribution force. The corpus specifically tracks the operational role of Alibaba Cloud in hosting these models and in supporting enterprise and government adoption of Qwen across Asia, the Middle East, and parts of Africa.
