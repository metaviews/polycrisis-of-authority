# Google (Alphabet)

Alphabet Inc. is the parent company of Google DeepMind, Google Cloud, and the historic Google Brain research organization (merged into DeepMind in 2023). Through these entities, Google is the principal competing commercial counterpart to OpenAI in the frontier-AI race and one of the structural anchors of the global AI industry. The Gemini family of models is Google's primary public-facing frontier output; the Google Cloud compute infrastructure is a key substrate for third-party AI developers; the Anthropic partnership (Google's multi-billion-dollar investment in Anthropic alongside Amazon's) gives Google a parallel frontier-model capability.

The relationship between Alphabet and Google DeepMind has structural specificity. Alphabet acquired DeepMind in 2014; the 2023 merger of DeepMind with Google Brain consolidated the company's AI research under one roof. Anthropic is independently operated (founded 2021 by former OpenAI researchers) but receives substantial Google compute and capital — Google's positioning as both an in-house frontier-model developer and a capital partner in independent frontier labs is structurally distinct from Microsoft's exclusive OpenAI partnership.
