# Meta AI

Meta AI is the artificial-intelligence research division of Meta Platforms (Facebook), forming out of Facebook AI Research (FAIR), the company's research arm with substantial existing investment in transformer-based language modeling. Meta AI's primary public product is the Llama family of language models, with Llama 2 (released July 2023) and Llama 3 (April 2024) anchoring a distinct open-weight distribution strategy. Meta has historically been one of the most public frontier-lab commiters to open-weight releases, partly because a closed-platform business model (advertising revenue, social network positioning) makes open-weight distribution less competitively costly than for API-based business models.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus references Meta AI as one of the principal open-weight frontier providers, alongside Mistral, Alibaba/Qwen, and DeepSeek. The "open-weight stack" frame in the corpus includes Meta as a contributor whose policy posture is distinct: open-weight release for researcher and enterprise use, combined with the company's policy positioning on AI safety (Yann LeCun's public commentary on "open and reproducible AI," Joe Pineiro's safety-policy frameworks).
