# Microsoft

Microsoft Corporation is the principal commercial partner of OpenAI through the multi-year partnership structure announced in 2023 and refined in successive relationships throughout 2024-2026. The partnership has three structural components: (1) a substantial commitment of Microsoft Azure compute infrastructure to OpenAI workloads — the compute substrate OpenAI depends on for both training and inference; (2) Microsoft's exclusive license to OpenAI's intellectual property for commercial deployment through Microsoft's enterprise channels and the Azure OpenAI service; (3) Microsoft's investment in OpenAI corporate equity.

Through 2025-2026, Microsoft's role has evolved. The acquisition of the Inflection AI team (March 2024, with Mustafa Suleyman becoming EVP and CEO of Microsoft AI) gave Microsoft a parallel in-house frontier-model capability. The Azure OpenAI service became the principal Microsoft AI offering for enterprise customers; competing frontier labs (Anthropic, xAI, Cohere) became available on Azure alongside OpenAI. Microsoft's overall positioning is that of a platform provider rather than a model developer — Azure as the substrate for the AI industry, with multiple vendors and models running on top.
