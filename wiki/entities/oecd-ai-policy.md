# OECD AI Policy Observatory

The OECD (Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development) is the intergovernmental organization of 38 member countries, founded in 1961. Its AI work includes the **OECD AI Principles** (first adopted 2019, updated 2024) — one of the first intergovernmental AI ethics frameworks and still widely cited — and the **OECD AI Policy Observatory**, which serves as a policy hub tracking national AI strategies across member and partner countries.

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus uses the OECD as one of the principal references for "intergovernmental coordination on AI policy" — alongside the UN AI Advisory Body and the G7 Hiroshima AI Process. The OECD's AI Principles (Inclusive growth, Human-centered values, Transparency, Robustness, Accountability) are cited when simulations need a widely-recognized principles framework.
