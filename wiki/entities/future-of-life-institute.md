# Future of Life Institute

The Future of Life Institute (FLI), founded in 2014 by Max Tegmark and others, is a US-based civil-society research and advocacy organization focused on existential risks from AI. The institute's principal artifacts are the **Asilomar AI Principles** (2017), a set of 23 principles covering research, ethics, and values for the development of advanced AI; the **AI Safety Camp** educational program; and a long run of policy briefs engaging with frontier-AI regulation.

FLI's positioning in the AI policy conversation has been distinct from AI Now Institute's. AI Now's framing is accountability-grounded (focusing on AI's social and economic effects, infrastructure critique, and immediate accountability gaps). FLI's framing is existential-risk-grounded (focusing on the longer-horizon question of how to ensure advanced AI does not produce catastrophic outcomes). Both organizations conduct policy-research work; both are civil-society voices; they emphasize different framings.
