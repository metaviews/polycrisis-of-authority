# US Congress

The US Congress — composed of the Senate (100 members) and the House of Representatives (435 members) — is the legislative branch of the US federal government and the body constitutionally empowered to pass binding federal law. AI-related legislative activity has grown substantially since 2023, with multiple hearings in both chambers each year and a steady stream of proposed bills addressing sectoral AI disclosure (election-related, content authenticity, biometrics), procurement rules, evaluation requirements, and AI's intersection with existing regulated industries (housing, lending, healthcare, education).
