# Frontier Model Forum

The Frontier Model Forum is an industry-led coalition of leading AI labs formed in July 2023, originally with Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI as founding members, with Meta and other companies joining in 2024. The Forum's commitments include advancing AI safety research, promoting responsible frontier-model development practices, facilitating information-sharing with policymakers and civil society, and supporting effective AI applications. Its institutional positioning is the industry-coordinated counterpart to multi-stakeholder bodies (Partnership on AI) and government bodies (AISI, NIST, EU AI Office).

## In the Polycrisis corpus

The corpus references the Frontier Model Forum as one of three distinct institutional framings of AI governance — industry coalition, multi-stakeholder nonprofit, government regulator — and uses the Forum when simulations need to depict industry-coordinated safety work as a distinct lever from regulatory intervention.
