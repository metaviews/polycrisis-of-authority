## Synthesis

Medical interventions ranging from cancer-detecting brain-computer interfaces to psilocybin therapy for Alzheimer’s are transforming the human central nervous system into a site of active engineering and data extraction. As neurosurgery experiments on pigs and microbiome transplants in mice blur the lines between biological aging and technical plasticity, the intimate architecture of thought and memory is being integrated into the broader infrastructure of algorithmic management and corporate care. This shift marks a transition from treating the mind as a private sanctuary to managing it as a hackable, predictive asset within a new economy of cognitive optimization.
